V2 — Couple Discovery & Engagement

This is the free, public-facing marketplace loop: a couple finding, comparing, and reaching out to vendors. Test both as a logged-out visitor and a logged-in couple — several features (favorites, enquiry submission) behave differently depending on auth state, and that difference is itself something to verify.
Before you start: you need at least 3-4 real, APPROVED vendors across at least 2 categories and 2 cities in the dev database to search against meaningfully (seed via admin panel or use existing seeded vendors). Have one couple account ready from V1.
Not applicable — don't test these, they don't exist: in-app chat/messaging with a vendor (WhatsApp deep-links are the real mechanism, tested here instead), guest-list manager, seating planner, budget-tracking tool, social/OAuth login.
