V1 — Auth & Account Foundation

Everything downstream (couple flows, vendor flows, admin) depends on accounts working correctly. Test this phase first, with both a fresh couple account and a fresh vendor account. Auth is email or phone + password only — there is no social login (Google/Facebook) and no OTP login in this platform, so don't hunt for those buttons; their absence is expected, not a bug.
Before you start: have at least 2 disposable test emails/phone numbers ready (you'll deliberately trigger duplicate-account and lockout scenarios, which will burn accounts). Use +test1-style email aliases if your provider supports it so you don't run out. Never test against real user data.
